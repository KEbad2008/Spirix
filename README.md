**How to Play --- Spirix**:

Visit https://spirix.netlify.app to start playing!

**Objective**:
Your goal is to survive as many waves as possible by destroying incoming metal enemies while avoiding taking damage.

There is no final level. The game continues until you die, and your goal is to reach the highest wave and score possible.

**Controls**:
Use your mouse to maneuver.
Your sawblade automatically spins and damages enemies on contact
Hold shift to constrict your sawblade and heal your health.
Hold the space-bar to expand your sawblade to reach mobs easier without taking damage.

**Basic** **Gameplay**:
You control a spinning sawblade inside an arena
Enemies spawn in waves and will chase you
Touch enemies with your blade to destroy them
Avoid letting enemies or bullets touch your core body

**Waves**:
Each wave introduces more enemies than the previous one
Enemies gradually become stronger and more dangerous
You must eliminate all enemies to progress to the next wave

**Health and Death**:
You have a limited amount of health
Taking damage from enemies reduces your health
If your health reaches zero, the game ends immediately

**Tips**:
Keep moving at all times to avoid being surrounded
Try to guide enemies into your blade’s path instead of chasing them
Do not let multiple enemies surround you at once
Early waves are easier; difficulty increases steadily over time

**Goal**:
Survive for as long as possible and achieve the highest wave count and score!

Thank you and enjoy the game!
